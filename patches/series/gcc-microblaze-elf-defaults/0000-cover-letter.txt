microblaze: three more elfos.h defaults

Three settings that "microblaze: include elfos.h" kept, now replaced by
elfos.h's.  Applies on top of that patch.

1/3 MAX_OFILE_ALIGNMENT was 32768 bytes, so aligned (65536) on a
variable was an error although gas and ld handle much larger
alignments.  Use the ELF (1 << 28).  Fixes gcc.dg/attr-aligned-3.c.

2/3 Read-only data used gas's MicroBlaze-only ".rodata" directive;
".section .rodata" gives an identical object and lets the testsuite's
scan-assembler-symbol-section see the section (gcc.dg/array-quals-1.c,
20021029-1.c, pr25521.c).  microblaze_asm_output_ident built its
.pushsection from that directive and now uses section names; its output
is unchanged.

3/3 Local labels used the "$" prefix and a private
ASM_GENERATE_INTERNAL_LABEL from the MIPS port ($L5, $LC0, $LCFI0).
Use elfos.h's, with LOCAL_LABEL_PREFIX "." for the jump tables.  BFD
treats both prefixes as local, so the objects are unchanged apart from
the names of local symbols the assembler keeps.  Fixes gcc.dg/pr110729.c,
which expects .LPFE labels.

Tested on microblaze-elf under QEMU 10.2.4 with compile.exp, execute.exp,
dg.exp, dg-torture.exp and microblaze.exp: 29 FAILs become PASS
(attr-aligned-3.c 7, array-quals-1.c 20, 20021029-1.c, pr25521.c), and
no other result changes.  3/3 was tested separately on top of the
first two, with the same five sets big-endian and execute.exp and
microblaze.exp little-endian: pr110729.c's 3 FAILs become PASS, and no
other result changes.
