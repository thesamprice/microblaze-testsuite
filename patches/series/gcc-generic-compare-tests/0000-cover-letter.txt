testsuite: compare, conversion and small-struct-return tests

Five new tests, all generic, each written after a MicroBlaze bug that
the testsuite did not catch.  None needs target options.

1/5 float-int-compare-1.c compares (int) F with constants for F around
zero and one.  A MicroBlaze peephole2 dropped the conversion, so
(int) -0.5f < 0 was true.

2/5 compare-boundary-1.c and -2.c test every signed and unsigned
comparison of int and long long, between variables and against
constants, as a branch and as a value, at the sign and carry
boundaries.  Written while auditing MicroBlaze's compare-and-branch
expanders, which had two bugs that C could not reach.

3/5 gimplefe-62.c writes unsigned comparisons against zero in GIMPLE,
so that at -O0 they reach the cbranch expander as (gtu x 0) and
(leu x 0).  MicroBlaze's expander tested them as signed.

4/5 float-compare-1.c tests float and double comparisons and the
unordered built-ins on signed zeros, infinities and a NaN.  QEMU's
MicroBlaze fcmp.le and fcmp.ge compare their operands the wrong way
round, and no GCC test noticed.

5/5 reg-struct-return-1.c returns small structs and unions with
-freg-struct-return, which ICEd on MicroBlaze for one- and two-byte
aggregates.

The expected results in 1/5, 2/5 and 4/5 come from tables of ranks and
truncated values, so they do not use the operations under test.

Tested on microblaze-elf and microblazeel-elf under QEMU, on
arm-none-eabi (Cortex-M0, Cortex-M3, Cortex-M4 with FPU) under QEMU,
and natively on aarch64-linux-gnu with GCC 14.  Details below.

microblaze-elf and microblazeel-elf, QEMU 10.2.4, with the MicroBlaze
fixes for these bugs: every test passes at every torture level (78
results per endian).  With -mcpu=v10.0 -mhard-float -mxl-float-convert,
1/5 and 4/5 also pass, on a QEMU with fcmp and fint fixed.

Against unpatched gcc master df809a029:

  5/5 fails: 14 FAIL and 7 UNRESOLVED, the ICE, at every level.
  1/5 with -mcpu=v10.0 -mhard-float -mxl-float-convert: ICE at -O2 and
      -O3; with -fno-checking it compiles and aborts there.
  4/5 with -mhard-float on stock QEMU 10.2.4: aborts at every level, as
      does 1/5, because of QEMU's fcmp and fint bugs.
  2/5 and 3/5 pass: the bugs they were written for cannot be reached
      from C, and the middle end canonicalizes 3/5's comparisons
      before expand.  A MicroBlaze test with the same four functions
      aborted at -O0 on a scratch build with the gtu/leu
      canonicalization disabled.

arm-none-eabi-gcc 16.2.0 on QEMU (mps2-an385 and mps2-an386), Cortex-M0,
Cortex-M3 and Cortex-M4 with -mfloat-abi=hard -mfpu=fpv4-sp-d16, at -O0
to -Os and -O2 -flto: all 84 runs pass.

aarch64-linux-gnu, GCC 14.2.0, at -O0 to -Os: all pass.
