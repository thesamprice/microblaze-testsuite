testsuite: five fixes for targets that differ from the tests' assumptions

Five testsuite patches from the first run of compile.exp, dg.exp and
dg-torture.exp on microblaze-elf.  In each the compiler's output is
right and the test assumed something the target does not do.  Two are
generic, two widen a pattern or a target list, and one skips a single
torture level on MicroBlaze.

1/5 The hierarchical-discriminator tests look for discriminators in
.loc directives on gas targets.  A gas target can still have gcc write
the line table (DWARF2_ASM_LINE_DEBUG_INFO 0, as on MicroBlaze); pass
-gas-loc-support on gas targets so the directives are there.

2/5 fold-round-1.c's folds create calls to floorf and floorl, which
GCC does not do without the C99 runtime; require c99_runtime as the
builtins tests do.  mips-elf, riscv32-elf, sparc-elf and microblaze-elf
fail it the same way.

3/5 pr110729.c expects .LPFE labels; MicroBlaze's local labels start
with $.

4/5 MicroBlaze in pic-1.c (one PIC model, __PIC__ is 2),
ifcvt-4.c (no conditional move) and Wno-frame-address.c.

5/5 strub-run3.c at -O0 on MicroBlaze: the stack restore after an
alloca writes a saved word back over the end of the string the test
expects a non-strub function to leave behind.  The strub checks pass.

Tested on microblaze-elf under QEMU 10.2.4, with other MicroBlaze fixes
applied: the 15 FAILs these tests had become 10 PASS (the
seven discriminator tests and pr110729.c's three scans) and 5
UNSUPPORTED (fold-round-1.c, pic-1.c, ifcvt-4.c, Wno-frame-address.c,
and strub-run3.c at -O0); no other result changed.  With newlib,
builtins-config.h does not define HAVE_C99_RUNTIME, so fold-round-1.c
is UNSUPPORTED on the other newlib targets too.
