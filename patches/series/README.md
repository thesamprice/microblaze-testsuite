# Patch series for upstream

Each directory here is a patch series as it will be mailed upstream: a
`0000-cover-letter.txt` and the `git format-patch` output. The Makefile
does not apply them. A series the build should use is also copied into
`patches/<component>/`.

| series | for | status |
|---|---|---|
| `binutils-microblaze-opcodes` | binutils@sourceware.org | seven patches against binutils master `1b10c36dfd4`, not yet sent. Fixes the `bslli`/`bsrai`/`bsrli` decoding, `wdc.ext.clear`, the `mbar` pseudo-instruction aliases and the zero word, comments the `mbar` forms that enter a sleep mode, removes the undocumented `pcmpbc`, and adds a test of every instruction whose expected encodings were checked against QEMU and UG984. Issues #40 to #44 in thesamprice/rtems-microblaze-linker-relaxation-bug |
| `dejagnu-spawn-split` | dejagnu@gnu.org | one patch against DejaGnu master `6ea0ff7`, not yet sent. DejaGnu master since 2026-11-06 passes the whole command line to `spawn` as one word, so no local command with arguments runs; this splits it again. Issue #72 in thesamprice/rtems-microblaze-linker-relaxation-bug. Also in `patches/dejagnu/`, without the ChangeLog entry |
| `gcc-generic-compare-tests` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | five generic tests against gcc master `df809a029`, not yet sent, each written after a MicroBlaze bug the testsuite missed: float-to-int compares (#54), integer compares at the sign and carry boundaries, unsigned compares against zero in GIMPLE (#76), float compares with NaN and signed zeros (#60), and `-freg-struct-return` (#56). Pass on MicroBlaze, Cortex-M0/M3/M4 and aarch64 |
| `gcc-microblaze-generic-moddi3` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | three patches against gcc master `df809a029`, ready to send. Makes `__udivdi3` and `__divdi3` mask out the quotient bits, adds a remainder-only loop for `__umoddi3`/`__moddi3` that a target opts into with the documented `LIBGCC2_MOD_WITHOUT_QUOTIENT`, and has MicroBlaze opt in and drop `moddi3.S`. Figures are estimated cycles. Tested big- and little-endian and with the divider; nothing slower on Cortex-M0. Also in `patches/gcc/`. GCC 17 stage 1 ends 2026-11-16, so post before then |
| `gcc-microblaze-zero-fails` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | four patches against gcc master `df809a029`, not yet sent. Fixes `va_arg` in a C23 `void f (...)` and an ICE with `-fPIE -mpic-data-is-text-relative`, makes the `gcc.target/microblaze` tests pass, and makes the `fileio` effective target run its program. With them `execute.exp` and `microblaze.exp` have no failures, big- and little-endian. Also in `patches/gcc/` |
| `gcc-microblaze-cbranch` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | three patches against gcc master `df809a029` plus `gcc-microblaze-zero-fails`, not yet sent. Removes `cbranchsi4_reg`, never generated and with its EQ branch inverted; adds run tests for the integer compare-and-branch patterns; and stops unsigned comparisons against zero being tested as signed (#76, latent). From the audit after #54: the fint peephole2 was the backend's only peephole. Also in `patches/gcc/` |
| `gcc-microblaze-libgcc-asm` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | two libgcc patches against gcc master `df809a029`, not yet sent, from the audit of the hand-written MicroBlaze routines: remove the unused `muldi3_hardproc`, wrong on little-endian (#77), and make `-mxl-stack-check`'s `_stack_overflow_exit` exit with status 1 through `_exit` (#78). Also in `patches/gcc/` |
| `gcc-microblaze-generic-divsi3` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | **on hold**, not sent. One libgcc patch against gcc master `df809a029` replacing the hand-written `divsi3.S`, `modsi3.S`, `udivsi3.S` and `umodsi3.S` with the generic C. A trade-off: faster on average and for short quotients, but about 50-59% slower for long quotients and in the worst case. Also the `mulsi3.S` and `__muldi3` measurements (keep `mulsi3.S`). Not in `patches/gcc/`. See its NOTES.md for the follow-up options and how to recreate the numbers |
| `gcc-microblaze-carry` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | three MicroBlaze back-end patches against gcc master `df809a029`, ready to send. 64-bit shifts by one, 32-bit shifts by 31 and unsigned 64-bit overflow checks go through the carry flag. Independent of the libgcc series, but its division speed-ups show only with it: with both, `__divdi3` without a divider drops from 1717 to 524 estimated cycles per call on the 5-stage pipeline with no branch target cache, and from 1301 to 412 with a perfect one. Shrinks the default multilib's libc by 7.7%. Tested big- and little-endian. Not in `patches/gcc/`, so `make` builds without it |
| `gcc-microblaze-audit-fixes` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | eight MicroBlaze back-end patches against gcc master `df809a029` plus the other gcc series here, not yet sent, from the 2026-11-15 audit: compare-and-swap succeeding when values differ in bit 31 (#81), the GOT pointer for TLS on bare metal (#82), a blockage before the epilogue releases the alloca area (#83), interrupt handlers saving sp and their CFI return column (#86), iprefetch in delay slots (#100), latent pattern bugs and `atomic_test_and_set`'s result (#102), the `-mxl-stack-check` note (#103), and an ICE with `-fPIC -msmall-divides -mxl-barrel-shift` (#106). 0004 needs `gcc-microblaze-fixes` 0002; the other seven also apply to bare `df809a029` and are in `patches/gcc/` as 0013-0019 |
| `gcc-microblaze-opt` | gcc-patches@gcc.gnu.org | three MicroBlaze code-quality patches against gcc master `df809a029` plus the other gcc series here, not yet sent (#113 items 1, 3, 5): 64-bit add immediates with `addi`/`addic`, `lwr`/`swr` for byte-reversed accesses, and shift costs without a barrel shifter. Does not apply without `gcc-microblaze-fixes`, `-elfos`, `-carry` and `-pcmpbf`, so not in `patches/gcc/`. Tail calls are not included |
| `binutils-microblaze-relax-fixes` | binutils@sourceware.org | nine linker-relaxation patches, not yet sent, on binutils master `7b7c85d7f65` plus the in-review relax-safe CFI series (0001-0015), `binutils-microblaze-64-none`, `-no-keep-memory`, `-srec-64/on-relax-cfi`, `-eh-relax` and `-opcodes` (#84, #85, #88, #89, #90, #98, #101, #104, #105). Adds `R_MICROBLAZE_ALIGN` (46), which older ld rejects; RTEMS libdl needs `rtems-microblaze-libdl-relocs`. First of three, then `-local-labels`, then `-gas-fixes`. Not in `patches/binutils/`: the runner does not carry the series underneath |
| `binutils-microblaze-local-labels` | binutils@sourceware.org | one patch (#114), a fixup for "gas: microblaze: emit ADD and SUB for code label differences" and "gas: microblaze: emit CFA advances as relocations" of the in-review relax-safe CFI series, to fold into their v2; also fixes readelf's SUB relocation with an addend. Applies after `binutils-microblaze-relax-fixes` |
| `binutils-microblaze-gas-fixes` | binutils@sourceware.org | nine gas and opcodes patches, not yet sent, after `binutils-microblaze-local-labels` (#87, #91-#97, #99): crashes, aborts and silent truncation become errors (lowercase `@plt`/`@got` and `@GOT`/`@PLT` in data now error), line rows at the IMM prefix, `.8byte`, `@GOTOFF` in `.word`, objdump symbols at or above 0x80000000 |
| `rtems-microblaze-tls-get-addr` | GitLab rtems/rtos/rtems | one patch against RTEMS `53d56d9494`, not yet sent (#82): `__tls_get_addr` returns the thread pointer plus the offset, so extern `_Thread_local` works. Needs the GCC half, `gcc-microblaze-audit-fixes` 0002. RTEMS not built; the logic is tested in GCC's `tls-run.c` |
| `rtems-microblaze-libdl-relocs` | GitLab rtems/rtos/rtems | one patch against RTEMS `53d56d9494`, not yet sent (#115): the libdl MicroBlaze loader accepts the ADD/SUB/SET/ULEB128 (36-45) and ALIGN (46) relocations of our binutils patches. dl05 loads and passes with it, with and without relaxation, on `petalogix_s3adsp1800` (little-endian) |
